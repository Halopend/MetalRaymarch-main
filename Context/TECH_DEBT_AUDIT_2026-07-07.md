**Priority = (Impact + Risk) × (6 − Effort)**, each 1–5 (Effort 5 = hardest).

dependencies/docs/infra) over the current working tree, cross-checked against the

and (c) a fresh priority ranking + phased plan.

  *(Fixed this session.)*
- **git-SHA stamp only on the visionOS target** → Mac baselines (where benchmarks are actually captured) are unattributable.

- **Phase 0 — Safety net & hygiene (½–1 wk, first):** A, B, C, G. Cheap, unblock everything; without a gate every later fix can silently regress.
- **Phase 1 — De-risk the surfaces that bit (1–2 wks):** D, I, J, F. Exactly the paths behind recent incidents (CloudKit freeze, import auto-dismiss) + the overdue occupancy measurement.
- **Phase 2 — Kill duplication seams (2–4 wks, incremental):** H (top ROI), E, K, N, M, O. H removes a three-way hand-sync; N/M feed the menu-scroll perf work.
- **Phase 3 — Structural, needs go/no-go (quarter-scale):** P, Q, L + the Swift-6 concurrency reckoning. Sequence after the rebuild decision.

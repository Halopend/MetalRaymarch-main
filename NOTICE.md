Threshold does not claim ownership of a user's original input or creative work

The GPL covers program output only when the content of that output is itself a
covered work. Ordinarily, original scene settings, artwork, renders,
screenshots, video, and audio created by a user may be licensed by that user on
terms of their choice.

An output file can nevertheless contain material that already has license
terms. In particular:

- a new formula created in Metal DE Studio begins with project-provided starter

- a scene or formula copied from a bundled sample can retain project or

- imported music, formulas, models, artwork, or other content retains the terms

Those incorporated portions keep their applicable terms. Replace covered
starter or sample material with independently authored material if you need an
export that does not incorporate it. The author or distributor of a file is
